Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026. Replace the bracketed items before launch.

What we collect

When you register we collect your name, email, phone number, chosen course and, optionally, your gender and address. The contact form collects your name, email and message. The newsletter collects your email address.

Why we collect it

To process your registration, contact you about your course, reply to your questions and, if you subscribe, send learning updates.

Who handles it

Your details are stored with our service providers: Supabase (database and login) and Formspree (email delivery of form submissions). Optional spam protection and analytics are provided by Cloudflare. If you use the live chat (Tawk.to) your messages and basic browser details are processed by Tawk.to, and if you tap the WhatsApp button your chat is handled by WhatsApp (Meta). We do not sell your data.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or unsubscribe at any time, by using the contact form [or emailing your-address@example.com].

Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies. If you log in, your browser stores a session token so you stay signed in. The live chat widget may set its own cookies to recognise returning chats.
